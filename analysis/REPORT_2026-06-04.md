## 2. Entry-window analysis

Open+30m wins in both pairs by an almost identical margin, and the V1-vs-V3 gap *widens* to +$0.45 in the since-April regime. Each comparison alone is below 95% significance; the credibility is in the **consistency across two profit targets and two time periods**.

➡️ **Best entry window = Open + 30 minutes = 15:00 UK (BST) / 10:00 ET.**

**Profit-target axis** (window held constant): 10% PT beats 20% pooled (+$0.48 vs +$0.32/trade) with a much higher win rate (68.9% vs 56.6%). Optimal cell = +30m entry + 10% PT = **Iron Fly V1**.

Near-symmetric (skew +0.04), shallow max drawdown (−$5.85, recovered in 8 trades), exceptional Calmar (9.16). Excess kurtosis 3.18 = contained fat tails (the settles).

### 4.2 The exit decomposition — this is the whole story
| Exit reason | n | W/L | Avg | Total |
|---|--:|---|--:|--:|
| **PROFIT-TARGET** | 56 | 56/0 | +$1.33 | **+$74.37** |
| TIME-STOP (18:00) | 23 | 6/17 | −$0.46 | −$10.57 |
| SETTLED (held to expiry) | 6 | 0/6 | −$1.70 | −$10.20 |

Net +$53.60 = **+$74.37 (profit target) − $10.57 (time-stops) − $10.20 (settles)**. The 10% profit target is the entire engine — 56/56 hits are wins, reached in a median of **58 minutes**. The two loss buckets are the cost of doing business: stalled trades caught by the 18:00 time-stop, and the rare position that can't be closed and settles (always a loss, worst −$4.25).

### 4.3 Time-of-exit clustering — "the longer it holds, the worse it gets"
| Hold-to-exit | W/L | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 30–60m | 18/0 | pure wins |
| 1–2h | 19/0 | best winners (+$1.69 avg) |
| 2–3h | 7/0 | pure wins |
| **3–4h** | **15/16** | **the battleground — coin-flip** |
| Held to settle | 0/5 | always loses |

Everything exited before 3h is ~100% wins; **all the losses live in the 3–4h+ zone**. The fast 10% target and the time-stop are doing exactly the right job.

### 4.5 Independence
Lag-1 autocorrelation +0.046; win% after a winner (72.6%) ≈ after a loser (72.7%). Trades are essentially independent — no streak-chasing or martingale logic is justified.

### 4.6 Data caveats specific to V1
- **IV-regime cut is unreliable.** The `IV Rank` column mixes 0–1 ratio and 0–100 percentage encodings (V1 values span 0.13 → 67.2), so `strategy_metrics`' IV bins split on encoding, not vol. No IV conclusion drawn.
- **Worst trade is a bug artifact.** The −$4.25 on 2026-02-20 is a stale-trade auto-expire (`"Stale 0DTE: auto-expired (prior day)"`), not a clean loss. Excluding it, V1's worst real outcome is ~−$2.00 and the tail is even tamer.
- **SPX day-move cut is sparse** for V1 (only the 6 settled trades carry a parseable close), so it's not representative.

### 4.7 Improvement hypotheses (backtest before changing — strategy change = plan mode)
1. **Defend the settles.** The 6 held-to-settle trades (0/6, −$10.20, worst −$4.25) are the worst outcomes. A defensive stop or a forced close before settlement *might* trim the tail — but the 3–4h bucket is +$1.23 net, so a blunt "force-close at 3h" would also cut winners. Needs a backtest.
2. **Keep the 10% target.** It is the edge; do not widen it (10% already beats 20% across the board).
3. **Don't add a DOW filter** — every day is positive.
